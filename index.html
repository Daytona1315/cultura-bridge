About Us

Born at HSE University

Cultura Bridge started at the HSE University Moscow campus. Founded by two students of intercultural communication, we were inspired by everyday conversations with peers from around the world.

We realized that real learning happens beyond textbooks—in real encounters, misunderstandings, and shared discoveries. That's why we built a platform where difference only bridges, not divides.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about how Cultura Bridge helps you master communication.

Q. What is Cultura Bridge?

Cultura Bridge is a mobile app designed to help you master intercultural communication. It combines educational theory with interactive games, quizzes, and roleplay scenarios to prepare you for real-world interactions abroad.

Q. Who is this app for?

It's perfect for students preparing for exchange programs, business professionals working in international teams, and travelers who want to go deeper than just being a tourist.

Q. Is the app free to use?

Yes! The core learning experience is completely free. We also offer a "Cultura+" subscription for power users who want advanced analytics, early access to new features, and profile customization.

Q. Do I need internet access?

Most core lessons can be downloaded for offline use, perfect for learning on the plane. However, community features and live updates require an internet connection.
