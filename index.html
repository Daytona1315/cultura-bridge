About Us

Born at HSE University

Cultura Bridge started at the HSE University Moscow campus. Founded by two students of intercultural communication, we were inspired by everyday conversations with peers from around the world.

We realized that real learning happens beyond textbooks—in real encounters, misunderstandings, and shared discoveries. That's why we built a platform where difference only bridges, not divides.

Our Values

At Cultura Bridge, we believe curiosity is the key to connection. We value empathy and open-mindedness.
